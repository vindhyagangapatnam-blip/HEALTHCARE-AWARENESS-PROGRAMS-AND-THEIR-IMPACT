📄 Abstract

Community health plays a vital role in improving the overall quality of life and preventing common illnesses. As part of our 6-week Community Service Project from SREE VENKATESHWARA ENGINEERING COLLEGE, NORTHURAJUPALAM, conducted at NELLORE (CHAKALI STREET SACHIVALAYAM 44/2), our team carried out door-to-door surveys, awareness campaigns, and interactive sessions on personal hygiene, balanced nutrition, sanitation, and seasonal disease prevention. The primary objective of this initiative is to identify local health challenges, educate residents about preventive healthcare measures, and bridge the gap between local communities and essential public health resources.

🧩 Diagrams & Modules

Module 1: Field Survey & Data Collection

Module 2: Data Analysis & Problem Identification

Categorizing collected data to identify prevalent health concerns such as seasonal fevers, nutritional deficiencies, and hygiene gaps.

Module 3: Awareness & Educational Campaigns

Organizing community talks, distributing pamphlets, and demonstrating proper handwashing and water purification methods.

Module 4: Feedback & Impact Assessment

Re-visiting households in the final week to evaluate behavioral improvements and document community feedback.

🏥 Health Program & Activities

Hygiene & Sanitation Drive

Educated residents on waste segregation, clean surroundings, and proper handwashing techniques to curb infections.

Nutrition & Diet Counseling

Conducted awareness sessions for women and children on iron-rich foods, balanced diets, and maternal nutrition.

Seasonal Disease Prevention

Guided families on preventing dengue, malaria, and waterborne diseases through mosquito control and safe drinking water.

📚 Content & Modules (Subtopics: Week 1 – Week 6)

Week 1: Community Orientation & Baseline Survey

Selected the target locality at NELLORE (CHAKALI STREET SACHIVALAYAM 44/2), obtained permissions from local authorities, designed the health survey questionnaire, and initiated door-to-door visits across 30 households to gather baseline health and sanitation data.

Week 2: Personal Hygiene & Safe Drinking Water

Analyzed survey data regarding water usage. Conducted demonstrations on boiling/filtering water, safe storage practices, and proper handwashing habits for school children and elders.

Week 3: Maternal, Child Health & Nutrition

Focused on balanced nutrition using affordable local foods. Educated mothers on anemia prevention, immunization schedules, and child growth monitoring.

Week 4: Vector-Borne & Seasonal Disease Awareness

Organized an awareness rally and pamphlet distribution on preventing dengue, malaria, and typhoid. Highlighted the importance of eliminating stagnant water around homes.

Week 5: Lifestyle Diseases & First-Aid Basics

Conducted interactive sessions for middle-aged and elderly residents on blood pressure, diabetes management, regular walking, and basic home first-aid kits.

Week 6: Impact Evaluation & Final Documentation

Conducted a post-awareness follow-up survey, compiled statistical charts comparing pre- and post-program awareness levels, and prepared the final project report.
